# Privacy Policy — FerrisFeed

Effective: October 5, 2026. Contact: privacy@ferrisfeed.app.

FerrisFeed teaches Rust offline. This policy is short because we collect
very little.

**What we store on your device.** Your XP, quiz grades, mastery scores,
streak, and scroll position stay in on-device storage (Room + DataStore).
Uninstalling the app deletes them. We cannot see them.

**Optional anonymous usage stats.** If enabled (default on, toggle in
Settings -> Privacy), we receive: reel IDs viewed, dwell-time buckets,
save actions, and quiz grades per topic. No reel text, no code you write,
no name, email, location, contacts, or advertising ID. Purpose: fix bad
reels and measure whether spaced repetition works. Retention: 14 months,
then auto-deleted.

**Opt-out.** Settings -> Privacy -> "Usage analytics" off stops all
transmission immediately. Previously sent aggregates cannot be
un-singled-out (they contain no identifier), but Firebase deletion
requests are honored per Google's Data Deletion process.

**Crashes.** Anonymous crash reports (stack trace, OS version, app
version) via Firebase Crashlytics to fix bugs. No memory contents.

**Network.** The app ships its 400+ reel curriculum inside the APK and
works fully offline. The weekly curriculum sync contacts
cdn.ferrisfeed.app over HTTPS to fetch new packs; standard server logs
apply. No third-party ad or tracking SDKs.

**Children.** FerrisFeed is a general programming reference, not directed
at children under 13, and collects no personal data from anyone.

**Changes.** Material changes will be noted in the release notes and the
in-app privacy screen before they take effect.
